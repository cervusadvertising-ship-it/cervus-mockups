Ask about a bundle

Questions owners usually ask.

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. I make your homepage mockup before we talk money. If you don't like it, you don't owe anything.

Who owns the website and the domain?

You do. Your domain is registered in your name, and if you ever leave, I hand everything over.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, a list of your services, your hours and service area, 5–10 photos, and a few reviews you like. I send a simple checklist, and I can supply professional stock photos if you're short.

How many changes can I ask for?

Starter includes one round of revisions, and Business and Premium include two. A round means you send all your changes in one list and I make them.

Where is the site hosted, and what does it cost?

Simple business sites run on fast, low-cost hosting. I'll tell you the exact cost before we start, and there are no surprise fees.

Can I update the site myself later?

You can, or a care plan covers it so you never have to. Most owners would rather send a text and have it done.
